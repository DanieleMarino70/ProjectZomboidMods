description=[h1]Survival Instinct[/h1]
description=[i]"Fear sharpens your reflexes."[/i]
description=
description=Adds a [b]6 point[/b] positive trait: when zombies are closing in, you learn to fight faster.
description=
description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=Real danger [b]enables[/b] the bonus, emotion [b]amplifies[/b] it:
description=[list]
description=[*]Safe, with no zombies around: [b]no bonus[/b]. Ever.
description=[*]With zombies nearby: the melee XP multiplier scales with how many there are and how close they get.
description=[*]Panic and stress push it higher still, up to [b]x2.5[/b].
description=[/list]
description=Affected skills: Axe, Long Blunt, Short Blunt, Short Blade, Long Blade, Spear.
description=The trait also grants [b]+1 starting level[/b] in Long Blunt and Short Blade.
description=
description=[h2]Works with Desensitized and Veteran[/h2]
description=The bonus does not depend on panic alone, so it still works for characters who never panic. A Veteran stays cool-headed and still learns under real pressure, reaching 50 percent of the maximum bonus.
description=
description=[h2]Balance[/h2]
description=6 points is the vanilla price of Brawler, which gives the same 2 skill boosts and nothing else. Here the dynamic mechanic comes on top, and the trait is worth nothing at all when you play it safe.
description=
description=[h2]Changelog[/h2]
description=[b]1.2.0[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]The bonus now requires real danger: panicking in a safe house no longer gives any advantage.
description=[*]Added a "threat" channel based on nearby zombies, so the trait also works with Desensitized / Veteran.
description=[*]XP boosts cut from 5 skills to 2 (Long Blunt, Short Blade): five unconditional boosts were too much for a trait at this price.
description=[*]Cost raised from 4 to 6, in line with Brawler.
description=[*]Added Short Blunt to the boosted skills: hammers and crowbars previously got nothing.
description=[*]The multiplier now applies at skill level 0 as well.
description=[*]Updated for Build 42.20.
description=[/list]
